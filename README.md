## Test cells

The jobs of the test workflows, by the platform in their name. A web column holds every host and compiler: the tooltip says which. An extra target (a detail in the name, such as `Linux x64 fake camera`) is one more mark in its platform column, after the usual job. The unnamed columns hold every other job of a package, one each, labelled with its name: extra targets with no such column, and names with no platform. Unit holds the jobs of the unit workflow that name no platform.
